# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-30

Costed baseline and research round 1. Verdict: **NO_GO**; the holdout was not
opened.

### Added

- Explicit cost model (ADR 0007): market `max(2 bps, 1 tick)` adverse, stop
  5 bps adverse plus the bar gap, limit fills only after one tick through the
  price with 90% touch probability, commission/slippage stress multipliers.
- Funding coverage validation: missing or gappy funding aborts a run instead
  of silently assuming zero.
- Partitioned signal and run datasets by symbol/month; the transient SQLite
  journal is deleted after the parquet export by default.
- Frozen data split and gates (ADR 0006), holdout opening policy (ADR 0008),
  append-only experiment ledger, preregistered hypotheses H1-H3.
- Passive post-only entry at the zone edge, entry timeout/cancel handling, 1h
  signal configuration, dual-timeframe state routing.
- Baseline report, stress run, hypothesis walk-forward runner, gate
  re-evaluation and H1 adverse-selection analysis.
- Continuous loss audit: `docs/audits/why_no_edge.md` and the append-only
  `kaizen/audit_history.parquet`, regenerated by every validation run.

### Results

- Costed baseline, dev-A: 103 trades, gross -3.53, slippage 3.30, fees 4.25,
  net -7.85, PF 0.767; the 50% kill switch triggers on 2025-01-19. The old
  optimistic model showed 240 trades and +2.79 gross before fees; the old
  edge was a later, luckier slice of a path the realistic model did not
  survive.
- Four-fold baseline: 253 trades, net -34.77, PF 0.625, expectancy -0.1697R,
  0/4 positive folds; the random benchmark is better (-0.1482R).
- H1 best (h1a): 678 trades, PF 0.919, -0.0296R, stress PF 0.860.
- H2: ratio 4.0 gives 27 trades; 5.0+ gives zero.
- H3 best (h3b): 89 trades, PF 0.884, -0.0391R, stress PF 0.795.
- No configuration passes the frozen gates; the holdout remains sealed.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-29

Research system, first complete pass through Milestone 6.

### Added

- Framework-independent core: UTC bar aggregation (1m -> 15m/1h/4h), Wilder
  ATR, confirmed pivots with R-bar delay, ATR zone clustering, sweep buffer,
  ADX + Choppiness regime, range scoring, scanner, decision engine.
- Range V1 strategy plus trend-pullback and seeded random-entry benchmarks.
- Fee/slippage-aware position sizing with exchange minimums, leverage, margin
  and liquidation checks; account risk policy with daily loss limit, losing
  streak cooldown and drawdown kill switch.
- Binance public data downloader (checksum verified, resumable), canonical
  parquet normalizer, point-in-time frozen universe, instrument metadata, data
  quality report, Nautilus catalog export.
- NautilusTrader 2.0.0rc8 backtest adapter: low-level engine runner and a
  catalog-driven `BacktestNode` path with a golden regression test.
- Funding accounting reconstructed from the fill timeline because the pinned
  release drops `PositionAdjusted` before Python; account-vs-trades residual is
  reported per run.
- Append-only SQLite journal, DuckDB/Parquet ETL, rule-based error tags,
  post-exit drift, missed-opportunity analysis, ambiguity audit.
- Validation suite: walk-forward, parameter sensitivity, ablations, benchmark
  strategies, Monte Carlo (iid and block bootstrap), bootstrap confidence
  intervals, PSR/DSR and a pre-registered GO/NO_GO gate.
- Streamlit dashboard with seven pages and unit tests.
- Docs: architecture, Nautilus v2 notes and gaps, exchange constraints,
  backtest assumptions, runbook, five ADRs.

### Known limitations

- Live execution is not implemented (gated on GO).
- Bar execution cannot resolve intrabar stop-vs-target ordering; ambiguity is
  counted and reported.
- Backtest stops trigger on `LAST`, live must use `MARK_PRICE` and be validated
  on testnet.
- No latency model; bar-only latency would defer fills by a full bar.
